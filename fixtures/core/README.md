Synthetic fixtures for the C++ scanner core's tests. They include photos of made-up MyBizMate Sales Invoices and Service Invoices written in handwriting fonts, with their variants. Each comes with what the Python reference (prototype/benchmark) reads in it. Also here: live camera frames, and pages with their Enhanced and B&W views. No client data. Regenerate from prototype/benchmark with the project's venv: `python synth_service_invoice.py`, then `python export_core_fixtures.py`.

pdf/, bir2307/ and expected2307/ hold made-up BIR Form 2307s (page 1) as text PDFs, picture PDFs and photos, the Sales Invoice as a picture PDF and a typed text PDF, and a broken, a password-protected and a two-page PDF. Each readable one has an expected2307/<stem>.json with the values written in it (synth_2307.py, which export_core_fixtures.py runs).
